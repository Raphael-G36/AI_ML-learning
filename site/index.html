About the house

The Sizzling Bistro is a premium grill house in the heart of Victoria Island. Every dish comes off live flame — steaks rested and finished with garlic butter, burgers charred to order, and a short menu that changes only when the fire tells us to.

We keep it simple: real cuts, honest prices, and a room that feels like an occasion even on a Tuesday.
